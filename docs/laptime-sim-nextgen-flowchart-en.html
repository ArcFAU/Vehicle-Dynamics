Laptime Simulation nextGen

System and data flow of the quasi-static lap-time simulation – from the vehicle concept and local performance limits to energy, event points, and sensitivities.

InputsVehicle physicsQSS-SolverEnergyEvaluationdashed = decision / optional

1 · Configuration

Select and preflight the vehicle, track, event, explicit model ID, strategy, and study parameters.

createCase → validateCase

2 · Vehicle model

Combine tires, aerodynamics, powertrain, suspension, chassis, and control into performance limits.

OUT: vmax(κ), ax±(v, ay)

3 · QSS solver

Initialize the cornering limit and form a feasible speed profile through iterative forward and backward passes.

OUT: v(s), ax(s), ay(s), t(s)

4 · Energy

Post-process the completed profile or directly couple SoC and battery power back into it.

OUT: SoC, kWh, Ppeak

5 · Decision

Compare lap time, event points, energy demand, and parameter sensitivity.

OUT: concept evaluation
Fast path: dualTrackEnvelope / direct → normal solver → energy post-processing.
Detailed path: explicit slip model / trackLocal or mapped → audit → optional SoC coupling.
